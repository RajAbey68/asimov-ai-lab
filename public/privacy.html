Privacy Notice

This notice covers the ASIMOV AI website at asimov-ai.org. It is written in plain English under UK GDPR.

Who we are

ASIMOV AI. We provide independent AI risk counsel to professional services firms. Contact: advisory@asimov-ai.org.

What we collect

When you submit the diagnostic form we collect: your name, firm, role, email address, and your answers to the intake questions (sector, headcount, your stated AI concern).

Why we collect it

One purpose only: to prepare for and schedule your 30-minute consultation. We do not use it for anything else.

Lawful basis

Legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry, and your consent, which you give by ticking the box before submitting. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

Retention

We delete your form data within 12 months of submission, or earlier on request.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties. We do not use it to train AI models.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email advisory@asimov-ai.org and we will act on the request. You can also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
