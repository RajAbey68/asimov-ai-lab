Executive AI Jargon Dictionary

Plain-English Translations for Practice Heads

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Searching your firm's private confidential document vault in real time to answer queries with precise citations, without retraining or exposing internal files to public models.

Zero Data Retention (ZDR)

A strict contractual mandate requiring AI vendors to process confidential inputs entirely in memory and permanently discard data immediately without caching or training.

Blast Radius

The maximum potential damages across your firm if an autonomous AI agent makes an erroneous decision or executes unauthorized actions.
