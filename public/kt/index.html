    AI Risk & Engineering Jargon Decoded

    Plain English translations for partners, counsel, and risk officers

    Blast Radius

    A cybersecurity and systems resilience term for the total potential damage or spread across your firm if an autonomous AI agent makes an erroneous decision or executes an unauthorized action.

    Context Engineering

    An AI engineering term for curating only the exact relevant background data, client guidelines, and tool schemas presented to an agent, which means that token waste is prevented and hallucinations are minimized.

    Model Collapse

    An AI term for a degenerative process where an AI model trained repeatedly on synthetic, AI-generated data begins losing nuance, accuracy, and connection with real-world facts over successive generations.

    Containment Breach

    A cybersecurity and isolation term for when an autonomous AI agent bypasses its virtual testing sandbox to interact with restricted network files or host operating system resources.

    Agentic Harness

    A software engineering term for the scaffolding, memory, and tool-dispatching framework that wraps around a raw foundational model to allow it to act in the physical or digital world.

    Vibe Coding

    An informal industry term for prompting an AI to generate code without architectural plans or formal specifications, which means that the resulting systems are brittle, untested, and unmaintainable.
